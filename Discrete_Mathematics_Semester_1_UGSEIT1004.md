## Course Objective

The objective of this course is to develop a strong foundation in discrete mathematical structures such as sets, relations, functions, and logic. It aims to enhance analytical thinking and problem-solving skills essential for computer science. The course emphasizes logical reasoning and its application in computational contexts.

---

---

## Course Outcomes

After successful completion of the course, students will be able to:

- **CO1:** Apply set theory concepts and operations to represent and solve mathematical problems.
- **CO2:** Analyze and classify relations and functions, and apply their properties in problem-solving.
- **CO3:** Evaluate logical statements using propositional calculus and construct valid logical arguments.

## Note for Paper Setting

The External End Semester Theory Examination will have a total of **40 marks**.

The question paper will be divided into **two sections: Section A and Section B**.

### Section A

- Section A will contain **04 compulsory short-answer-type questions**.
- At least one question will be included from each unit.
- Each question will carry **2.5 marks**.

### Section B

- Section B will contain **06 long-answer questions**, with **02 questions from each unit**.
- Students will have to attempt **01 question from each unit**.
- Each question will carry **10 marks**.
